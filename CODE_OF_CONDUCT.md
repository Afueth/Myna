# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in myna to be a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be welcoming, patient and respectful.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its community.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults or derogatory comments.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers at **afueththomas@outlook.com**.
All reports will be reviewed promptly and kept confidential. Maintainers may
remove, edit or reject contributions, and may temporarily or permanently ban
contributors who violate this code.
